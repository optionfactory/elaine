Dashboard Governance Security Inventory

Scanner failures

Repositories where a scanner could not complete (e.g. OSV unreachable, npm/cargo/mvn/go not available or failing, unparseable lockfiles). Their vulnerability and outdated-dependency counts below are unknown, not zero: treat these numbers as missing until the check succeeds again.

Tier × exposure matrix

Live repositories classified by operational criticality (tier) and network exposure (most permissive ingress across their environments). The top-left corner, high tier on the open internet, is where a vulnerability has the highest chance of turning into an incident, so it deserves attention first. Cell intensity reflects the combined tier × exposure weight.

Known vulnerabilities (top 50)

Live repositories with known vulnerabilities, sorted by count. The same advisory often hits many repositories at once, so fixing a shared dependency once can clear multiple rows.

Outdated dependencies (top 50)

Live repositories by number of outdated dependencies, plus the artifacts that are stale across the organization. An artifact lagging everywhere is one upgrade campaign, not one ticket per repo.
